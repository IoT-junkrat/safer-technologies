How FLARE works

Swipe. Vote. Travel. The fastest social loop you've ever experienced.

Step 01
📸

Join a Trend

Take on the daily challenge or pick from dozens of trends — sunsets, street food, muscle cars, anime, hiking … Shoot at a real place: FLARE saves where your photo was taken.

Step 02
⚡

Battle & Vote

Two photos. One swipe. Swipe up or down to pick your favorite — every vote you give also earns you pushes (10 votes = 1 push).

Step 03
🗺️

Push & Travel

Visit a friend's photo spot and push it: +50 km reach instantly. Photos can hop from city to city — the more support, the further.

Photos that travel. Friends that explore.

The further your friends go, the further your photo can spread — from your block to other cities and, with enough momentum, across the world.

📍

Go there

Friends' photos appear on the map with a 1 km push zone. Get close — or hop on a train, plane or bike.

🚀

Push it

Give the photo an instant +50 km reach boost. Earn pushes by voting; up to 5 a day.

🌍

Watch it travel

With every push and vote it reaches more people — first nearby cities, then the whole country and beyond.

See it in action

Battles, trends, rankings — and your own travelling photos.

Made for explorers
and communities.

FLARE starts hyper-local and rewards people who go places. Content earns its way to a wider audience through votes and through friends who visit and push it. No algorithm gatekeeping — people decide who travels.

🏆

Local Leaderboards

Climb the rankings in your city, region, or country. See who's dominating your neighborhood.

🔥

Push & Radius

Votes widen your photo's reach, local pushes boost it instantly. From your block to other countries and continents.

🎯

Daily Challenge & Streaks

A new theme every day, with a streak flame to keep. Weekly winners get the spotlight.

🎁

Invite friends, get pushes

Share your code: when a friend joins in, you both get bonus pushes. Bring your crew to your city's top spot.
