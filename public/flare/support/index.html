Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started
FLARE is a social photo-battle app. You submit photos to trending challenges in your area and compete in 1v1 battles where the community votes. The more votes ("Flares") your photo earns, the wider its geographic reach spreads — from your neighborhood to your city to the whole country.
Download FLARE from the App Store, open it, and tap "Create Account". You'll need a username, email address, and password. No phone number required.
Yes. FLARE is free to download and use. The free version is supported by ads. A Premium subscription removes ads, unlocks the ability to create your own Trends, and gives access to additional profile customization.
  • Tap the + Create tab at the bottom of the app.
  • Browse active Trends near you and choose one.
  • Take a photo with your camera or pick one from your library.
  • Confirm and submit — your photo enters the battle queue immediately.
Battles & Voting
In the Home feed you'll see two photos from the same Trend side by side. Tap the one you prefer to cast a "Flare" vote. The next battle loads automatically. There's no limit to how many battles you can vote on.
Every photo starts with a small geographic radius — only people in your immediate area see it in battles. As it earns more Flares (votes), the radius expands: Neighborhood → City → Region → Country. High-performing photos can eventually be seen nationally.
No. Your own photos never appear in your battle feed. You cannot vote on or interact with your own submissions.
Account & Privacy
Location is used to show you local Trends and Battles relevant to your area, and to assign your photos a starting geographic radius. We only request location while the app is open — we never track you in the background. You can disable location in iOS Settings and manually enter your city instead.
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email address and we'll send you a reset link. If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact flare@safer.network.
Tap the ⋯ menu on any photo or profile and select "Report". Choose the reason that best describes the issue. Reports are reviewed by our moderation team. For urgent matters (e.g. illegal content), email flare@safer.network directly.
On iOS, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → FLARE and toggle off "Allow Tracking". You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Premium & Purchases
Go to Profile → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're logged in with the same Apple ID used to purchase. If the issue persists, contact us with your Apple Order ID.
Subscriptions are managed through Apple. To cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FLARE → Cancel Subscription. You'll retain Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

🗑️ How to Delete Your Account

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time (GDPR Art. 17). There are two ways to do this:

Option 1 — In-app (instant):

  1. Open FLARE and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (top right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm deletion. Your account and all data are permanently removed.

Option 2 — By email:

Send a deletion request to flare@safer.network from the email address registered to your account. We will process your request within 30 days.


Note: Some data (e.g. purchase receipts) may be retained for legally required periods even after account deletion. See our Privacy Policy for details.
