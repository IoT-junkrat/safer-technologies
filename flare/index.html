How FLARE works

Three seconds. Tap. Done. The fastest social loop you've ever experienced.

Step 01
📸

Join a Trend

Browse active photo trends in your area — "Outfit Check", "Waldvibes", "Haustier Battle" and more. Pick one and submit your best shot.

Step 02
⚡

Battle & Vote

Two photos. One tap. Swipe through an endless feed of 1v1 battles. Vote for your favorite and keep the loop going.

Step 03
🗺️

Spread Your Radius

Every Flare (vote) you earn expands your photo's reach — from your neighborhood to your city, region, and beyond.

community.
